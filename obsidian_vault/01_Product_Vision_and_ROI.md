# Product Vision & High School Pool Deck ROI

Back to [[00_PoloBridge_MOC]] | Hardware: [[02_Rev25_Hardware_and_PCB]]

> [!important] Why High School Pools Still Need PoloBridge
> Over 85% of US high school and club water polo pools run legacy **Colorado Time Systems System 6** or **Daktronics All Sport 5000** consoles with corroded brass deck plates and \$165 wired "pickle" switches that lack a dedicated **20-second reset plunger** (mandated by NCAA / World Aquatics and used in club/JO tournaments).

## 3-Tier Commercial Model
1. **Open-Hardware DIY (\$24.40–\$49.85)**: Full Gerbers, BOM, CPL, and `.STL` files in repo.
2. **High School Turnkey Kit (\$279 One-Time)**: Pre-assembled IP67 [[02_Rev25_Hardware_and_PCB|Rev 2.5 Dongle]] + cables + free iOS app (zero recurring subscription for public school booster clubs).
3. **Tournament & 6-8 Cloud Pro (+\$99/yr Optional)**: 4-Pool Multi-Course Command Hub + live 6-8 Sports cloud stat sync.
